Project Brief

scored using Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE) — lower is better.

Description

The DSN Mart Sales Prediction Hackathon is the qualifying competition for the DSN AI Bootcamp. Participants will work with historical retail sales data from DSN Mart to build a machine learning model that predicts the total sales of a product at a specific store.

The challenge is designed to assess participants’ ability to understand real-world data, identify important patterns and relationships, engineer useful features, and develop predictive models that can support business decision-making.

What the Challenge Involves

Participants will:

Explore and understand the provided product-store sales dataset.
Analyse factors that may influence product sales across different stores and locations.
Clean and preprocess the data.
Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) to identify useful patterns.
Engineer relevant features from the available product and store information.
Build and evaluate machine learning models.
Generate predictions for the required test data.
Submit their predictions to the competition platform and compete on the leaderboard.
Qualification for the DSN AI Bootcamp

This is not just a learning exercise; it is a qualification hackathon for the DSN AI Bootcamp.

Participants' leaderboard performance will be one of the inputs considered when selecting candidates for the Bootcamp. Therefore, participants are encouraged to focus on both the quality of their solution and their understanding of the problem.

Important: Participation in the hackathon does not automatically guarantee selection for the DSN AI Bootcamp. Your performance and rank will contribute to your chances of being selected.

What Participants Are Expected to Demonstrate

The hackathon provides an opportunity for participants to demonstrate their ability to:

Understand → Analyse → Model → Predict → Communicate

Success will depend not only on building a model, but also on applying sound data science practices to a real-world business problem.

In summary: Participants will use historical DSN Mart product-store data to build a model that predicts sales, submit their predictions, and compete on the leaderboard. Their performance will form part of the qualification process for the DSN AI Bootcamp.

Evaluation

For each row in test.csv, predict the value of total_sales. This is a regression problem. Your model's predictions are compared against the true sales values using Root Mean Squared Error (RMSE): lower is better.

Dataset Description

train.csv contains historical records with the true total_sales for each row. test.csv contains the same kind of records with total_sales removed — that's what you're predicting.

ColumnDescription	
id	Unique row identifier
product_code	Unique code for the product
product_weight_kg	Weight of the product in kilograms (some values are missing)
fat_content	Whether the product is labelled "Low Fat" or "Regular"
shelf_visibility	The proportion of total display area in the store allocated to this product
product_category	The product's category (note: capitalization is inconsistent — that's real-world data, not a bug)
product_price	The product's listed price
store_code	Unique code for the store
store_age_years	How long the store has been operating, in years
store_size	The store's size category: Small, Medium, or Large (some values are missing)
store_location_tier	The tier of the store's location — Tier_1 (major urban centers), Tier_2 (state capitals), Tier_3 (smaller towns)
store_format	The store's format: Corner Shop, Standard Supermarket, Superstore, or Flagship Hypermarket
total_sales	Target. Total sales value for this product at this store (train only)
